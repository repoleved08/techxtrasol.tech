## The Challenge

Janda Haven, a premium bed and breakfast, struggled with an outdated website that failed to capture the warmth and elegance of their hospitality. Their digital presence didn't match the quality of their service, resulting in missed booking opportunities and low online visibility.

### Key Issues

- Outdated design that didn't reflect premium hospitality standards
- Poor mobile experience with slow load times
- No online booking or inquiry system
- Weak SEO presence for local hospitality searches
- No integration with travel platforms or review sites

## Our Approach

### Discovery Phase

We started by understanding Janda Haven's brand identity, target guests, and competitive landscape. Through stakeholder interviews and competitor analysis, we identified the key differentiators that needed to shine through the digital experience.

### Design System

Created a warm, inviting design system that mirrors the physical hospitality experience. Color palettes drawn from natural tones, typography that balances elegance with readability, and photography-first layouts that let the property speak for itself.

## Technical Implementation

### Performance First

Built with Nuxt.js for server-side rendering and static generation, ensuring every page loads instantly. Image optimization with automatic WebP conversion and lazy loading keeps the experience smooth even on slower connections.

### SEO Strategy

Implemented structured data for hospitality, local business schema, and optimized meta tags. The site now ranks for key hospitality searches in the region.

## Results

- **95+** Core Web Vitals score
- **3x** increase in booking inquiries
- **1st page** rankings for local hospitality keywords
- **40%** improvement in mobile engagement
