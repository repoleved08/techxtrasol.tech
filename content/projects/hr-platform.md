## The Challenge

Human resource management in mid-size organizations often relies on a patchwork of spreadsheets, paper forms, and disconnected systems. This creates inefficiencies, data inconsistencies, and compliance risks.

### Operational Pain Points

- Manual attendance tracking prone to errors and buddy punching
- Payroll preparation requiring days of manual calculation
- Leave management through email and paper forms
- No real-time visibility into workforce metrics
- Compliance risks from inaccurate records

## Our Solution

### System Architecture

Built a modular HR platform with clear separation between attendance, payroll, leave, and analytics modules. Each module communicates through well-defined APIs, allowing independent scaling and maintenance.

### Biometric Integration

Integrated ZKTeco biometric devices through their SDK, enabling real-time attendance syncing. The system handles device communication, attendance record reconciliation, and exception management.

### Payroll Engine

Automated payroll calculation engine that considers:
- Base salary and allowances
- Overtime calculations
- Leave deductions
- Tax computations
- Statutory deductions (NHIF, NSSF)

## Technical Implementation

### Attendance System

Real-time attendance tracking with biometric verification:
- Device SDK integration for multiple biometric models
- Offline attendance queuing with sync on connection
- Shift management and overtime calculation
- Exception handling for missed punches

### Leave Management

Configurable leave management with:
- Multiple leave types (annual, sick, maternity, etc.)
- Approval workflows with escalation
- Leave balance tracking
- Calendar integration

## Results

- **70%** reduction in manual HR workload
- **99.5%** attendance accuracy
- **3x** faster payroll preparation
- **Real-time** workforce analytics
- **100%** compliance with labor regulations
